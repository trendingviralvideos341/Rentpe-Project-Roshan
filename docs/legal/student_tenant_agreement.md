## 2. RENT PAYMENT OBLIGATIONS

2.1 Your monthly **rent amount** (as agreed at booking) is due on the **billing date** specified in your booking confirmation each month.

2.2 Payment must be made through the Platform only using approved payment methods. Direct payments to the Owner outside the Platform are not permitted and shall not be considered valid against your Platform account balance.

2.3 You will receive a **monthly consolidated invoice** (accessible in your Tenant dashboard) prior to your billing date. The invoice will reflect rent, applicable food charges, and any credits applied.

2.4 In the event rent is not paid by the due date, a **grace period of up to 3 (three) days** will be provided. Thereafter, late fees shall apply as specified on the Platform.

## 3. SECURITY DEPOSIT

3.1 A **Security Deposit** equivalent to **1 to 2 months' rent** (as specified by the Owner at time of booking) is due before or at the time of booking confirmation.

3.2 The Security Deposit is held against potential damages, unpaid dues, cleaning costs, or any other legitimate deductions.

3.3 The Security Deposit does **not** accrue interest and is not to be treated as a rent payment.

3.4 **Refund of Security Deposit:** Subject to the below deductions, the deposit shall be refunded within **15–30 business days** of vacating the property, after deductions for:
   - Unpaid rent or food charges.
   - Physical damage to property or furnishings caused by the Tenant (beyond normal wear and tear).
   - Cleaning costs if the room is not vacated in reasonable condition.
   - Any penalties as specified in this Agreement.

3.5 The Platform will initiate refund processing upon the Owner confirming the move-out inspection. The final refund amount will be reflected in the Tenant's Platform account.

## 4. FOOD / MESS SERVICE BILLING

### 4.1 Types of Food Service

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **INCLUDED** | Meals are included in your monthly rent. You cannot opt out. |
| **OPTIONAL** | Meals are available as a paid monthly add-on. You can opt in or opt out. |
| **NOT AVAILABLE** | No food service is provided at this property. |

### 4.2 Price Lock

The food charge applicable to your booking is **locked at the price displayed at the time of booking confirmation**. The Owner may not increase this price without your consent. Any revised pricing will be communicated with a minimum notice of **15 (fifteen) days** and shall only apply from the next billing cycle.

### 4.3 Monthly Recurring Billing

Food charges (where applicable) are billed monthly on the same billing date as rent. They form part of your consolidated monthly invoice.

### 4.4 Proration (First Billing Cycle)

If your food service begins mid-month (e.g., you move in on the 15th), the food charge for the **first billing cycle only** will be prorated based on the actual number of days in the billing period. Subsequent cycles are billed as full monthly amounts.

### 4.5 Opt-In / Opt-Out Policy (OPTIONAL Food Only)

- You may **opt in to or opt out of** an OPTIONAL food plan at any time through the Platform.
- Changes will take effect from the **start of your next billing cycle**.
- No prorated refund will be issued for the current billing cycle if you opt out mid-month.
- Any food charges already invoiced for the current billing cycle are **non-refundable**.

### 4.6 INCLUDED Food Service

If the food service is listed as INCLUDED, it forms a mandatory part of your tenancy. You cannot opt out and no separate credit will be issued for meals you choose not to consume.

### 4.7 No Refund for Consumed Food

No refund shall be issued for any food billing period during which food service was active, regardless of whether meals were consumed.

## 5. LATE FEES AND PENALTIES

5.1 Late payment fee as displayed at the time of booking shall apply for each day beyond the grace period.

5.2 If dues remain outstanding for more than **15 (fifteen) days**, the Platform reserves the right to:
   - Restrict access to Platform features.
   - Notify the Owner.
   - Initiate recovery proceedings.

5.3 Repeated late payments (3 or more instances) may result in termination of your booking agreement.

## 6. PROPERTY RULES AND CODE OF CONDUCT

6.1 You agree to abide by the rules and regulations of the property as communicated by the Owner during onboarding or booking.

6.2 You must not:
   - Cause physical damage to the property, furniture, or equipment.
   - Create excessive noise or disturbance affecting other residents.
   - Engage in illegal activities on the premises.
   - Sublet or share your room without the Owner's express consent.
   - Bring prohibited items (as specified by the Owner) onto the premises.

6.3 **Breach of Rules:** Violation of property rules or legal misconduct will result in immediate termination of the booking. No refund shall be issued for the remaining part of the month in case of such termination.

## 7. EVICTION CONDITIONS

7.1 The Owner may initiate eviction proceedings in the following cases:
   - Non-payment of rent or dues for 15 or more days.
   - Serious or repeated breach of property rules.
   - Illegal or criminal activity on the premises.
   - Harassment of other residents or Owner/staff.

7.2 Eviction procedures will comply with applicable Indian landlord-tenant laws and the notice period agreed in the booking.

## 8. NOTICE PERIOD AND EARLY EXIT

8.1 You are required to provide **advance notice** as specified in your booking confirmation (typically 1 month) before vacating the property.

8.2 If you vacate the property before the end of your booking term without providing adequate notice:
   - The Security Deposit may be partially or fully forfeited.
   - You remain liable for rent until the notice period expires or a replacement tenant is found (whichever is earlier).

8.3 **No Carry-Forward of Rent:** No refund shall be issued for partial months of rent or food service. If you move out before the end of a billing cycle, the full billing cycle amount remains payable and non-refundable.

## 9. NON-REFUNDABLE SCENARIOS

The following are explicitly non-refundable:

- Food charges for any period in which food service was active.
- Any period of rent that has already been billed and invoiced.
- Security Deposit deductions due to damages, unpaid dues, or rule violations.
