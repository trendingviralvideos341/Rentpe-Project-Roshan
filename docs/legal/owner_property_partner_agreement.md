## 2. OWNER REPRESENTATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

2.1 **Accurate Listings:** You represent and warrant that all information provided in your property listing is accurate, complete, and not misleading. This includes:
   - Room descriptions, photographs, and amenities.
   - Price per room and applicable food charges.
   - Availability and operational status.
   - Regulatory compliance (fire safety, occupancy certificates, etc.).

2.2 **Regulatory Compliance:** You are solely responsible for ensuring your property complies with all applicable local, state, and national laws, including but not limited to:
   - Karnataka/local municipal licensing requirements for PG accommodations.
   - Fire safety and building occupancy norms.
   - RERA compliance (where applicable).

2.3 **Honoring Bookings:** Once a Booking is confirmed through the Platform, you must honour it. You shall not reject, delay, or cancel a confirmed booking without valid cause and proper notice to the Platform.

2.4 **Facilities as Listed:** You must provide the exact facilities, amenities, and services (including food) advertised. Any material change requires **15 days' notice** and may result in the Platform issuing involuntary Credit Notes to Tenants at your cost.

2.5 **Police Verification:** You are strictly responsible for conducting mandatory Police Verification of all Tenants as per local Karnataka state laws. RentPe is not liable for any Tenant-related security lapses.

## 3. FOOD SERVICE OBLIGATIONS

3.1 If you list your property with a **INCLUDED** food plan, you are obligated to provide meals to all Tenants for the duration of their stay.

3.2 If you list your property with an **OPTIONAL** food plan, you must provide meals to any Tenant who has opted in. You cannot:
   - Force Tenants to opt in without their consent.
   - Disable the food service without prior notice and Platform's approval.

3.3 Food pricing once set at the time of listing may only be changed with **15 (fifteen) days' written notice** through the Platform. Revised pricing applies only prospectively — from the next billing cycle.

3.4 You are solely responsible for the **quality, hygiene, nutritional value, and safety** of all food served. RentPe assumes no responsibility for food quality, contamination, or foodborne illness.

3.5 If food service is listed but not provided consistently, the Platform may issue Credit Notes to affected Tenants and deduct the equivalent amount from your Platform payouts.

## 4. SECURITY DEPOSIT HANDLING

4.1 The Security Deposit collected from a Tenant through the Platform is held by the Platform on the Owner's behalf.

4.2 Upon a Tenant's move-out, you must communicate the inspection result and any legitimate deductions to the Platform within **7 (seven) business days**.

4.3 The Platform will calculate the net refund to the Tenant based on:
   - Unpaid rent or food charges.
   - Documented physical damage claims (with photographic evidence).
   - Any agreed penalties.

4.4 Unjustified withholding of a Security Deposit may result in Platform penalties, suspension, and/or dispute proceedings.

5.1 **Onboarding Fee:** A one-time platform onboarding fee is charged upon approval of your property listing. The amount is communicated during onboarding and is payable before your listing goes live.

5.2 **Service/Commission Fee:** The Platform charges a service fee on each successful Booking or a monthly subscription fee, as specified in your onboarding agreement. This is deducted before payout transfers to your registered bank account.

5.3 Fee structures are subject to revision with **15 (fifteen) days' prior notice** to you via registered email or the Platform dashboard. Continued use of the Platform after the notice period constitutes acceptance of revised fees.

You must NOT:

6.1 **Overcharge Outside Platform:** Demand or collect any payment from Tenants outside of the Platform billing system (including additional deposits, side payments, or cash collections not sanctioned by the Platform).

6.2 **Misleading Listings:** Post false, misleading, or fraudulent property information, including fabricated reviews or inflated amenity claims.

6.3 **Discrimination:** Refuse to accept Tenant bookings on the basis of religion, caste, gender, language, or other protected categories under Indian law.

6.4 **Direct Solicitation:** Use Tenant contact details extracted from the Platform to solicit direct bookings or conduct transactions outside the Platform.

6.5 **Underreporting:** Misrepresent the number of occupants or active bookings to reduce Platform commission.
