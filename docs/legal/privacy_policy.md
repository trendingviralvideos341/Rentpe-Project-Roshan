## 1. INTRODUCTION

RentPe Technologies Private Limited ("RentPe," "We," "Us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, with whom we share it, and how you can exercise your rights.

This Policy is compliant with:
- The Information Technology Act, 2000 (IT Act)
- The IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 ("SPDI Rules")
- The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") guidelines
- RBI Guidelines on Digital Lending and Payments

## 2. DATA WE COLLECT

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Identity Data:** Full name, date of birth, photograph, gender.
- **Contact Data:** Email address, phone number, current and permanent address.
- **Account Data:** Username, password (encrypted), role (Student/Owner).
- **Identity Documents:** Aadhaar card, PAN card, passport, or other government-issued ID (for KYC/verification).
- **Property Data (Owners):** Property details, address, photographs, licence numbers, bank account details.
- **Payment Data:** We do not store sensitive card data (CVV, Pin). All payment processing is done by our PCI-DSS compliant partner (Razorpay) via secure tokenisation as per RBI guidelines. We store only transaction IDs, billing history, and tokenised references.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data:** Pages visited, features used, session duration, clicks.
- **Device Data:** IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers.
- **Location Data:** City/region inferred from IP address (general, not precise GPS).
- **Log Data:** Server access logs, error logs.

### 2.3 Cookies and Tracking Technologies
We use:
- **Essential Cookies:** Required for basic Platform functionality and security.
- **Preference Cookies:** Remembering your language, login state, and settings.
- **Analytics Cookies:** Aggregated usage analysis (via PostHog or equivalent).

You can control non-essential cookies through your browser settings or our cookie preference panel.

## 4. DATA SHARING

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your data only with:

4.1 **Payment Gateway Partners** (e.g., Razorpay): For processing transactions. They are required to use your data only for payment processing under their own privacy and security policies.

4.2 **Cloud Infrastructure Providers** (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud): For hosting and data storage.

4.3 **Analytics Providers** (e.g., PostHog): For aggregated usage analytics. Personal data is minimised.

4.4 **Other Users:** Owners may see Tenants' name, phone, and booking details for the purpose of managing the booking. Tenants may see the Owner's name, property address, and contact details.

4.5 **Law Enforcement and Regulatory Bodies:** When required by applicable Indian law, court order, or government authority.

4.6 **Professional Advisors:** Lawyers, auditors, and accountants under strict confidentiality obligations.

---

## 5. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

5.1 All data is stored on servers located in India or within jurisdictions that provide an adequate level of protection as required by Indian law.

5.2 We implement industry-standard security measures including:
   - AES encryption for data at rest.
   - TLS/HTTPS for data in transit.
   - Role-based access controls (RBAC) — only authorised personnel can access sensitive data.
   - Audit logs for all administrative actions.
   - Regular security assessments and penetration testing.

5.3 We practice a **no-hard-delete policy** for financial records. Invoices, credit notes, and payment logs are retained as permanent records as required by Indian accounting and tax laws.
