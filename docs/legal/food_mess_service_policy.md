## 1. OVERVIEW

This Food / Mess Service Policy governs how food and meal services are listed, billed, changed, and disputed on the RentPe platform. This Policy is binding on both Owners and Tenants.

---

## 2. TYPES OF FOOD SERVICE

RentPe supports three food service models:

### 2.1 INCLUDED

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Description** | Meals are provided as part of the monthly rent. |
| **Charges** | Built into monthly rent. No separate line item. |
| **Opt-Out** | Not allowed. Tenants cannot opt out of INCLUDED food service. |
| **No Credit** | No refund or credit is issued for meals not consumed. |

### 2.2 OPTIONAL

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Description** | Meals are available as a paid monthly add-on. |
| **Charges** | Billed as a separate line item on the monthly invoice. |
| **Opt-In** | Tenant must explicitly opt in via the Platform. No automatic enrollment. |
| **Opt-Out** | Tenant may opt out through the Platform. Change takes effect from the next billing cycle. |
| **Confirmation** | Owner may not activate OPTIONAL food for any Tenant without that Tenant's explicit consent (confirmable via Platform). |

### 2.3 NOT AVAILABLE

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Description** | No food service of any kind is provided at this property. |
| **Charges** | None. |
| **Owner Obligation** | Owner must not offer informal paid food services alongside a NOT AVAILABLE listing. |

## 3. PRICE LOCK

3.1 The food charge applicable to a Tenant is **locked at the price displayed at the time of booking confirmation**.

3.2 The Owner may not unilaterally increase the food price for an existing Tenant.

3.3 **Revision Notice:** Price revisions are only permitted with **15 (fifteen) days' advance notice** via the Platform. Any price change will only be applicable from the **next billing cycle** following the expiration of the notice period.

3.4 **No Retroactive Changes:** Under no circumstances can food pricing be changed for an invoice that has already been generated and locked.

---

## 4. MONTHLY BILLING

4.1 Food charges (OPTIONAL and INCLUDED where separately tracked) are billed monthly as part of the consolidated invoice alongside rent.

4.2 Invoices are generated on the **billing anchor date** set at the time of onboarding (typically the 1st of each month but configurable).

4.3 Once an invoice is generated and locked, the food charges therein **cannot be revised** except through a formal Admin-approved Credit Note process.

---

## 5. PRORATION — FIRST BILLING CYCLE

5.1 **First Cycle Only:** Proration applies exclusively to the Tenant's initial enrollment period if it starts after the billing anchor date.

5.2 **Proration Formula:**  
   `Prorated Amount = (Monthly Price / total days in the current month) * (days remaining in current cycle)`

5.3 **Subsequent Cycles:** All subsequent months following the initial enrollment cycle are billed at the full monthly rate, regardless of actual days of consumption.

---

## 6. OPT-IN / OPT-OUT POLICY (OPTIONAL FOOD ONLY)

6.1 **Opt-In:** A Tenant enrolled in the OPTIONAL food plan has confirmed their preference. The confirmation is recorded on the Platform with a timestamp.

6.2 **Opt-Out:** A Tenant may request to opt out at any time through the Platform.
   - The request is logged immediately.
   - The change takes effect from the **start of the next billing cycle**.
   - No partial refund is issued for the remaining days of the current billing cycle.

6.3 **Opt-Back-In:** A Tenant who has opted out may re-enroll in the OPTIONAL food plan through the Platform. On re-enrollment after the billing anchor date, proration applies for the remainder of the current cycle.

6.4 **Owner-Initiated Changes:** An Owner may request to enable or disable the OPTIONAL food service for a Tenant only through the Platform. The Tenant will receive a confirmation notification and the change will only be enacted upon Tenant approval.

---

## 7. FOOD NOT PROVIDED — ADMIN OVERRIDE AND CREDIT

7.1 If an Owner fails to provide food service for a period during which it was listed as active and billed to Tenants, the Platform Admin may:
   - Issue a **Credit Note** to affected Tenants equivalent to the unbilled food amount.
   - Deduct the equivalent amount from the Owner's future payouts.

7.2 Such an override must be supported by Tenant complaints and any available evidence.

7.3 The Owner may contest an Admin override through the Platform's dispute process within **7 (seven) days** of the override notice.

---

## 8. OWNER OBLIGATIONS FOR FOOD SERVICE

8.1 Owners offering food service are solely responsible for:
   - Menu quality, hygiene, and nutritional content.
   - Compliance with FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) regulations.
   - Allergen disclosure to Tenants.
   - Consistent and timely meal service.

8.2 RentPe is **not responsible** for food quality, safety, or any foodborne illness. All liability rests with the Owner.

8.3 Any food-related regulatory violation (e.g., FSSAI non-compliance) is the sole liability of the Owner and may result in Platform suspension.
