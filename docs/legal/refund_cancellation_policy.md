# REFUND AND CANCELLATION POLICY

**Last Updated:** [DATE]

---

## 1. OVERVIEW

This Refund and Cancellation Policy governs all refund and cancellation scenarios on the RentPe platform. It applies to Tenants, Owners, and all financial transactions processed through the Platform.

---

## 2. BOOKING CANCELLATION

### 2.1 Cancellation by Tenant (Before Move-In)

| Time of Cancellation | Refund Amount |
|----------------------|---------------|
| More than 30 days before move-in date | Full refund of Security Deposit. Platform fees non-refundable. |
| 15–30 days before move-in date | 50% of Security Deposit refunded. |
| Less than 15 days before move-in date | No refund of Security Deposit. |

### 2.2 Cancellation by Owner

If an Owner cancels a confirmed booking:
- The Tenant will receive a **full refund** of all amounts paid, including booking fees.
- The Owner may face Platform penalties, suspension, and liability for incidental costs incurred by the Tenant.

### 2.3 Cancellation by Platform

The Platform may cancel a booking due to fraud, erroneous listings, or force majeure events. In such cases, a full refund of amounts paid will be issued to the Tenant.

---

## 3. SECURITY DEPOSIT REFUND

3.1 The Security Deposit refund shall be processed within **15–30 business days** of the Tenant vacating the property.

3.2 The following may be deducted from the Security Deposit before refund:
   - **Unpaid Rent / Food Charges:** Any outstanding invoiced amounts.
   - **Physical Damage:** Verified damage to property or fittings (beyond normal wear and tear), supported by photographic evidence and Owner's report.
   - **Cleaning Costs:** If the room is not vacated in a reasonable state of cleanliness.
   - **Notice Period Shortfall:** If the Tenant vacates without honoring the required notice period, rent equivalent to the shortfall period may be deducted.

3.3 If the outstanding deductions exceed the Security Deposit amount, the Tenant remains liable for the balance.

3.4 The Tenant will receive an itemised **Refund Settlement Statement** via email within 7 business days of the Owner confirming the move-out inspection.

---

## 4. RENT REFUND

4.1 **No refund** will be issued for any portion of a billing cycle already invoiced and paid.

4.2 Rent paid in advance that corresponds to a period after the Tenant's confirmed move-out date (where applicable) may be refunded on a pro-rata basis at the Platform's discretion.

---

## 5. FOOD BILLING REFUND

5.1 **No refund** shall be issued for any food billing period during which the food service was active, regardless of:
   - Meals not consumed by the Tenant.
   - Short absence or travel during the period.
   - Personal dietary changes.

5.2 Food charges for a billing period in which the service was **not provided by the Owner** (despite being listed as active) will result in a **Credit Note** being issued to the Tenant for that period. Refer to the Food / Mess Service Policy for details.

5.3 If the food service was prorated for the Tenant's first billing cycle and the booking is subsequently cancelled before the first full month, the prorated amount may be refunded at the Platform's discretion.

---

## 6. CREDIT NOTES AND ADMIN OVERRIDES

6.1 In certain cases, the Platform's Admin team may issue a **Credit Note** to a Tenant instead of a direct cash refund. Credit Notes are applied against future invoices using a **FIFO (First In, First Out)** order.

6.2 Credit Notes may be issued for:
   - Food not provided by the Owner despite being invoiced.
   - Admin-approved corrections to billing errors.
   - Goodwill adjustments authorised by the Admin.

6.3 Credit Notes can be carried forward indefinitely against future invoices for the same booking. They are not transferable.

6.4 Cash refunds in lieu of Credit Notes are at the sole discretion of the Platform Admin.

---

## 7. NON-REFUNDABLE AMOUNTS

The following are **explicitly non-refundable** under all circumstances:
- **Platform Convenience/Booking Fee** charged at booking.
- Any food charges for an active billing period.
- Rent for any period already invoiced or in progress.
- Security Deposit amounts deducted for legitimate damages/dues.
- Platform Onboarding Fees paid by Owners.

---

## 8. REFUND PROCESSING

8.1 Eligible refunds are credited to the original payment instrument used by the Tenant.

8.2 The processing time after Platform approval is **5–10 business days** for UPI/Net Banking and **7–15 business days** for cards, depending on the issuing bank.

8.3 The Platform is not responsible for delays caused by banking intermediaries or payment gateways.

---

## 9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

9.1 **Amendments:** This Policy may be updated with **15 (fifteen) days' prior notice**.

9.2 **Finality:** All refund decisions made by the RentPe Admin team for issues related to Food Service defaults or billing errors are final and binding on the Owner and Tenant.

9.3 **Jurisdiction:** All refund disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of Bangalore, Karnataka.
